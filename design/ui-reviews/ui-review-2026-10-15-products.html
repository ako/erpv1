P2

  1. [P2] Products.Product_NewEdit — elements cmbProductCategory, txtName, product type, unit (Judged).
    Wrong: the wireframe draws Name, Product type, Category, Purchase unit and Unit as required (b4, b7, b9, b21, b23). In the app none shows a required marker, and Category is blank on the saved Journey Chair, so a product exists with no category although the design says it must have one (Product_Overview shows the empty Category cell).
    Root cause: Products.VAL_Product (MDL 405) checks name, unit, purchase-unit category, service/inventory and negative prices but not Product_Category; the combobox has no required setting and no required-indicator class. Whether Category is truly mandatory is an intent question for ba-agent.
  2. [P2] Products.Product_NewEdit — wireframe b3 image uploader and the ‘Vendor prices’ link (Judged).
    Wrong: the page has no product image area and no link to vendor prices; both are drawn. A user cannot attach a picture or reach vendor prices from the product.
    Root cause: not in MDL 407 (no image widget, no link button). The vendor-price overview belongs to a later module and the link may be a deliberate forward reference; the image entity is Products.ProductImage in the wireframe binding. Needs a decision: build, or record as deferred.
  3. [P2] Products.Product_Overview — element gridMain filters (Judged).
    Wrong: wireframe b1 asks for filters on type, can be sold, can be purchased and category, plus a search box. Live has only the Product type drop-down; with hundreds of products the user can neither search by name or reference nor narrow by category.
    Root cause: MDL 406 attaches dropdownfilter fProductType to one column only; Name, Internal reference, Category and the two booleans have no filter widgets.
  4. [P2] Products.UoMCategory_NewEdit — element lntxtFactor (textbox, DecimalPrecision 2) (Judged).
    Wrong: Dozens is stored as 0.0833333333 and displays as 0.08; the wireframe shows 0.0833. A conversion factor shown at two decimals misleads (1/12 reads as 1/12.5), and a user editing and saving this row may write the rounded value back, making quantity conversions wrong. Not verified at runtime.
    Root cause: factor and rounding share the same two-decimal format in MDL 403 (lines 84-85); a ratio needs more precision (the model value has 10 decimals).

Wrong: the review DB holds one fixture product with blank reference/category/cost and one pricelist with no rules, against two and two in the wireframes; three UoM and product categories against two. Root cause: seed (413) plus journey leftovers; not a model defect, but several wireframe fields (Internal reference, Cost, rule rows) were therefore not seen populated.

What the harness cannot see

Stage 3 proves only that the 15 journey assertions ran on the flows journeys/Products.journey.json walks; absent is not green. The harness cannot see any page off the literal click path: a journey that clicks a row never inspects pages it does not open, and Pricelist_NewEdit with rules, UoM editing a factor, and the category popup are not asserted for rendering. It cannot see visual and styling regressions even on a covered page, because text-presence and DB-state assertions pass equally on a styled and an unstyled render. It cannot see cell-level rendering such as truncated names, a clipped Unit type drop-down, a factor shown as 0.08, or the 390px layout.
