# Module: Partners
**Layer:** Common (master data)
**Responsibility:** One address book: customers, vendors, contact persons and addresses, bank accounts, and the business profile of app users.

_Generated by `pipelines/odoo/arch_blueprint.py` from the confirmed BRDs; edit brd_spec.py / brd_content.py / ui_spec.py._

## Entities

| Entity | Persistent? | Key attributes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| UserProfile | yes | DisplayName | Odoo res.users delegates to res.partner (_inherits). Mendix: Administration.Account for login/roles + this profile. |
| Partner | yes | Name, IsCompany, AddressType, Email, Phone, Mobile | Any customer, vendor or contact person — companies and their contacts/addresses. |
| PartnerTag | yes | Name, Color, Active | Free tags to classify partners. |
| BankAccount | yes | AccountNumber, HolderName, BankName, BIC, Active | Bank account of a partner (shown on vendor bills / customer payments). |
