# Module: Purchasing
**Layer:** Domain + Logic
**Responsibility:** RFQs to purchase orders with manager approval, vendor prices and purchase analysis.

_Generated by `pipelines/odoo/arch_blueprint.py` from the confirmed BRDs; edit brd_spec.py / brd_content.py / ui_spec.py._

## Entities

| Entity | Persistent? | Key attributes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| VendorPrice | yes | MinQuantity, Price, Discount, LeadTimeDays, VendorProductCode, VendorProductName | A vendor's price and lead time for a product (drives RFQ prices). |
| PurchaseOrder | yes | Number, Status, Locked, OrderDeadline, ConfirmationDate, ExpectedArrival | A request for quotation that becomes a (possibly approved) purchase order. |
| PurchaseOrderLine | yes | Sequence, LineType, Description, Quantity, UnitPrice, DiscountPercent | A product, section or note line of a purchase order. |
| PurchaseAnalysis | view entity (OQL) | — | Purchase lines with ordered/received/billed quantities and amounts per vendor/product. (replaces purchase.report) |
