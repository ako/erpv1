# Module: Invoicing
**Layer:** Domain + Logic (downstream)
**Responsibility:** Customer invoices, vendor bills, credit notes and payments with allocations; invoice analysis.

_Generated by `pipelines/odoo/arch_blueprint.py` from the confirmed BRDs; edit brd_spec.py / brd_content.py / ui_spec.py._

## Entities

| Entity | Persistent? | Key attributes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice | yes | Number, InvoiceType, Status, PaymentStatus, InvoiceDate, DueDate | Customer invoice / credit note or vendor bill / credit note (no journal entries). |
| InvoiceLine | yes | Sequence, LineType, Description, Quantity, UnitPrice, DiscountPercent | A product, section or note line of an invoice (no tax/receivable/payable journal items). |
| Payment | yes | Number, Direction, PartnerKind, Amount, PaymentDate, Memo | Money received from a customer or paid to a vendor, allocated to invoices. |
| PaymentAllocation | yes | Amount | new: Mendix-native replacement of account.partial.reconcile / matching numbers. |
| RegisterPaymentRequest | no (non-persistent) | Amount, PaymentDate, Memo, Method | Wizard data: register a payment for one or more posted invoices. |
| CreditNoteRequest | no (non-persistent) | Reason, ReversalDate | Wizard data: reverse a posted invoice into a credit note. |
| InvoiceAnalysis | view entity (OQL) | — | Posted invoice lines with untaxed/total amounts and residual per partner/product/period. (replaces account.invoice.report) |
