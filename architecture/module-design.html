Module design — erpv1

Odoo 18.0 → Mendix 11.15 POC · Stage 3 sign-off artifact (modularize-domain.md) · generated by pipelines/odoo/arch_render.py from the confirmed BRDs F001–F008 · one Mendix app (Stage 0).

Proposal: 7 modules in a strict layer stack (associations only point downwards)

Invoicing → Inventory → Purchasing → Sales → Partners → Products → Core
Association graph is acyclic (checked by brd_generator.py, mutation-tested). 53 cross-module associations, every one pointing to a lower layer. No module exceeds 15 persistent entities (largest: Products, 8).

Sanctioned upward calls (user actions on an order trigger the downstream bridge microflow, as Odoo's sale_stock / purchase_stock / sale→account do):

  • Purchasing -> Inventory: SUB_PurchaseOrder_Approve -> SUB_PurchaseOrder_CreateReceipt
  • Sales -> Inventory: ACT_SalesOrder_Confirm -> SUB_SalesOrder_CreateDelivery

Platform modules used: System, Administration (accounts, roles), Atlas Core (UI), Data Widgets (grids/filters), Workflow Commons (approval inbox), Charts (analysis pages) — buy/build per item is the fit-gap step that follows this sign-off.

layer 1

Core

Shared kernel: the company, its currency and countries, taxes, payment terms and document numbering.

Business processes
Company setup; tax and payment-term configuration; numbering for every document type.
Users / roles
Administrator, InvoicingManager (taxes, terms); read by everyone.
Boundary earned by
Bounded context "company configuration" used by every other module; sits at the bottom so nothing below it can create a cycle.
Rejected alternative
Keeping Tax and PaymentTerm in Invoicing (as Odoo does in `account`): Partners, Products and Company reference them, which made master data depend on Invoicing and closed a cycle. Moved here.
Depends on (associations)
—
Used by
Invoicing, Partners, Products, Purchasing, Sales

Owned data — 7 persistent entities, 6 enumerations

  • Company — The single company operating the app: identity, currency and document defaults.
  • Country — Countries for addresses.
  • Currency — Currency of the company and its documents.
  • Sequence — Document numbering per document type (S00001, P00001, WH/IN/00001, INV/2026/00001, ...).
  • LineAmounts non-persistent — Result of a line amount computation (subtotal, tax, total) passed between microflows.
  • Tax — A sales or purchase tax: percentage or fixed amount, price-included or not.
  • PaymentTerm — When an invoice must be paid: one or more instalments.
  • PaymentTermLine — One instalment: percent or fixed amount, due N days after a reference date.

Associations inside the module (5): Company_Country, Company_Currency, Company_DefaultSalesTax, Company_DefaultPurchaseTax, PaymentTermLine_PaymentTerm

Cross-module associations (0), all pointing down the layer stack: —

layer 2

Products

What we sell and buy: products, categories, units of measure and customer pricelists.

Business processes
Product catalogue maintenance; price determination for sales lines (F003 UC003); unit conversion (F002).
Users / roles
SalesManager, InventoryManager, PurchaseManager maintain; everyone reads.
Boundary earned by
Bounded context (catalogue & pricing, Odoo `product` + `uom`).
Rejected alternative
Vendor prices (Odoo `product.supplierinfo`) kept here: they point to vendors, which made Products depend on Partners while partners point to pricelists. Moved to Purchasing, where they are used. Separate UoM module: 2 entities, no criterion met.
Depends on (associations)
Core
Used by
Inventory, Invoicing, Partners, Purchasing, Sales

Owned data — 8 persistent entities, 6 enumerations

  • UoMCategory — Groups units that convert into each other (Unit, Weight, Volume, Time...).
  • UoM — A unit of measure and its conversion factor to the category reference unit.
  • ProductCategory — Hierarchical product grouping (used by price rules and reporting).
  • Product — A sellable and/or purchasable product or service (variants merged in).
  • ProductImage — Product picture.
  • ProductTag — Free tags on products.
  • Pricelist — A named set of price rules assigned to customers.
  • PriceRule — One pricelist rule: fixed price or % discount, for all/category/product, from a minimum quantity.

Associations inside the module (10): UoM_Category, ProductCategory_Parent, Product_Category, Product_UoM, Product_PurchaseUoM, Product_Tags, ProductImage_Product, PriceRule_Pricelist, PriceRule_Product, PriceRule_Category

Cross-module associations (3), all pointing down the layer stack: Product_CustomerTaxes → Core.Tax, Product_VendorTaxes → Core.Tax, Pricelist_Currency → Core.Currency

Logic: 6 microflows/workflows · Pages: 7 (Pricelist_NewEdit, Pricelist_Overview, ProductCategory_Overview, Product_NewEdit, Product_Overview, UoMCategory_NewEdit, UoMCategory_Overview)

layer 3

Partners

One address book: customers, vendors, contact persons and addresses, bank accounts, and the business profile of app users.

Business processes
Contact maintenance; address resolution for orders and invoices; user onboarding (profile + partner).
Users / roles
SalesUser, Purchaser, Billing maintain; SalesManager archives; Administrator manages users.
Boundary earned by
Bounded context (Odoo `res.partner`), referenced by every transactional module.
Rejected alternative
Merging into Core: partners are business data with their own UI and access rules, Core is configuration. UserProfile in Core: it links to Partner, which would make Core depend on Partners.
Depends on (associations)
Core, Products
Used by
Inventory, Invoicing, Purchasing, Sales

Owned data — 4 persistent entities, 1 enumerations

  • UserProfile — Business data of an app user (Mendix account): display name and linked partner. Team membership lives on Sales.SalesTeam.
  • Partner — Any customer, vendor or contact person — companies and their contacts/addresses.
  • PartnerTag — Free tags to classify partners.
  • BankAccount — Bank account of a partner (shown on vendor bills / customer payments).

Associations inside the module (4): UserProfile_Partner, Partner_ParentCompany, Partner_Tags, BankAccount_Partner

Cross-module associations (6), all pointing down the layer stack: UserProfile_Account → Administration.Account, Partner_Country → Core.Country, Partner_Salesperson → Administration.Account, Partner_CustomerPaymentTerm → Core.PaymentTerm, Partner_VendorPaymentTerm → Core.PaymentTerm, Partner_Pricelist → Products.Pricelist

Logic: 5 microflows/workflows · Pages: 4 (PartnerChild_NewEdit, Partner_NewEdit, Partner_Overview, UserProfile_Edit)

layer 4

Sales

Quotations to sales orders, quotation templates, sales teams and sales analysis.

Business processes
Quote → order (F004); triggers delivery (F007) and invoicing (F008).
Users / roles
SalesUser (own orders), SalesManager (all orders, lock, templates, teams, analysis).
Boundary earned by
Bounded context and security segregation (sales roles, own-documents rule).
Rejected alternative
One "Orders" module with sales and purchasing: different roles, approval rules and record rules; nothing shared but master data.
Depends on (associations)
Core, Partners, Products
Used by
Inventory, Invoicing

Owned data — 5 persistent entities, 2 enumerations

  • SalesTeam — Sales team with a leader and members (reporting, default on orders).
  • SalesOrder — A quotation that becomes a confirmed sales order.
  • SalesOrderLine — A product, section or note line of a sales order.
  • QuotationTemplate — Reusable quotation: default lines, validity and terms.
  • QuotationTemplateLine — A default line of a quotation template.
  • CreateInvoiceRequest non-persistent — Wizard data: create the invoice(s) for selected sales orders.
  • SalesAnalysis view entity — Order lines joined to order, customer, product, salesperson: amounts and quantities for pivots/charts.

Associations inside the module (5): SalesOrder_SalesTeam, SalesOrder_QuotationTemplate, SalesOrderLine_SalesOrder, QuotationTemplateLine_Template, CreateInvoiceRequest_SalesOrders

Cross-module associations (14), all pointing down the layer stack: SalesTeam_Leader → Administration.Account, SalesTeam_Members → Administration.Account, SalesOrder_Customer → Partners.Partner, SalesOrder_InvoiceAddress → Partners.Partner, SalesOrder_DeliveryAddress → Partners.Partner, SalesOrder_Salesperson → Administration.Account, SalesOrder_Pricelist → Products.Pricelist, SalesOrder_PaymentTerm → Core.PaymentTerm, SalesOrder_Currency → Core.Currency, SalesOrderLine_Product → Products.Product, SalesOrderLine_UoM → Products.UoM, SalesOrderLine_Taxes → Core.Tax, QuotationTemplateLine_Product → Products.Product, QuotationTemplateLine_UoM → Products.UoM

layer 5

Purchasing

RFQs to purchase orders with manager approval, vendor prices and purchase analysis.

Business processes
RFQ → PO (F005) incl. the approval workflow; triggers receipt (F007) and bills (F008); receives RFQs from reorder rules.
Users / roles
Purchaser, PurchaseManager (approval inbox, analysis).
Boundary earned by
Bounded context and security segregation (purchase roles, approval).
Rejected alternative
Purchase order pointing to the warehouse operation type and the reorder rule (Odoo `picking_type_id`, `orderpoint_id`): made Purchasing depend on Inventory while Inventory points to orders. Dropped / reversed (single warehouse).
Depends on (associations)
Core, Partners, Products
Used by
Inventory, Invoicing

Owned data — 3 persistent entities, 2 enumerations

  • VendorPrice — A vendor's price and lead time for a product (drives RFQ prices).
  • PurchaseOrder — A request for quotation that becomes a (possibly approved) purchase order.
  • PurchaseOrderLine — A product, section or note line of a purchase order.
  • PurchaseAnalysis view entity — Purchase lines with ordered/received/billed quantities and amounts per vendor/product.

Associations inside the module (1): PurchaseOrderLine_PurchaseOrder

Cross-module associations (9), all pointing down the layer stack: VendorPrice_Vendor → Partners.Partner, VendorPrice_Product → Products.Product, PurchaseOrder_Vendor → Partners.Partner, PurchaseOrder_Buyer → Administration.Account, PurchaseOrder_PaymentTerm → Core.PaymentTerm, PurchaseOrder_Currency → Core.Currency, PurchaseOrderLine_Product → Products.Product, PurchaseOrderLine_UoM → Products.UoM, PurchaseOrderLine_Taxes → Core.Tax

layer 6

Inventory

Warehouse, locations, transfers and stock moves, stock levels, counts and reorder rules — plus the order-fulfilment bridge.

Business processes
Receipts, deliveries, internal transfers, backorders (F006); delivery/receipt from orders (F007); replenishment.
Users / roles
InventoryUser (operations), InventoryManager (configuration, counts, rules).
Boundary earned by
Bounded context (Odoo `stock`); owns the links to sales/purchase orders exactly like Odoo `sale_stock`/`purchase_stock`.
Rejected alternative
A separate "Fulfilment" module for the bridges: 0 own entities, only associations and 4 microflows — over-split signal.
Depends on (associations)
Partners, Products, Purchasing, Sales
Used by
—

Owned data — 7 persistent entities, 3 enumerations

  • Warehouse — The (single) warehouse: its stock location and operation types.
  • Location — A physical or virtual place stock moves from/to (WH/Stock, Vendors, Customers, Inventory adjustment).
  • OperationType — Receipts, Delivery orders, Internal transfers: numbering and default locations.
  • Transfer — A receipt, delivery or internal transfer grouping stock moves.
  • StockMove — Movement of one product quantity between two locations (stock.move.line merged in).
  • StockLevel — Quantity of a product in an internal location (on hand, reserved, counted).
  • ReorderRule — Min/max replenishment rule: below minimum, create an RFQ up to maximum.
  • ValidateTransferRequest non-persistent — Wizard data: validate a transfer with less than the demand — create a backorder or not.
  • StockOnHand view entity — Per product: on hand, reserved, incoming and outgoing quantities (forecast).

Associations inside the module (16): Warehouse_StockLocation, Location_Parent, Location_Warehouse, OperationType_Warehouse, OperationType_DefaultSource, OperationType_DefaultDestination, Transfer_OperationType, Transfer_SourceLocation, Transfer_DestinationLocation, Transfer_Backorder, StockMove_Transfer, StockMove_SourceLocation, StockMove_DestinationLocation, StockLevel_Location, ReorderRule_Location, ValidateTransferRequest_Transfer

Cross-module associations (13), all pointing down the layer stack: Warehouse_Address → Partners.Partner, Transfer_Partner → Partners.Partner, Transfer_SalesOrder → Sales.SalesOrder, Transfer_PurchaseOrder → Purchasing.PurchaseOrder, Transfer_Responsible → Administration.Account, StockMove_Product → Products.Product, StockMove_UoM → Products.UoM, StockMove_SalesOrderLine → Sales.SalesOrderLine, StockMove_PurchaseOrderLine → Purchasing.PurchaseOrderLine, StockLevel_Product → Products.Product, ReorderRule_Product → Products.Product, ReorderRule_Vendor → Partners.Partner, ReorderRule_PurchaseOrders → Purchasing.PurchaseOrder

layer 7

Invoicing

Customer invoices, vendor bills, credit notes and payments with allocations; invoice analysis.

Business processes
Invoice/bill from orders, posting, credit notes, payment registration (F008).
Users / roles
Billing, InvoicingManager; SalesUser/Purchaser trigger invoice/bill creation from their orders.
Boundary earned by
Bounded context (Odoo `account`, invoices only) and security segregation (billing roles).
Rejected alternative
Full accounting with ledger, journals and reconciliation: out of scope (Q-F008-1).
Depends on (associations)
Core, Partners, Products, Purchasing, Sales
Used by
—

Owned data — 4 persistent entities, 7 enumerations

  • Invoice — Customer invoice / credit note or vendor bill / credit note (no journal entries).
  • InvoiceLine — A product, section or note line of an invoice (no tax/receivable/payable journal items).
  • Payment — Money received from a customer or paid to a vendor, allocated to invoices.
  • PaymentAllocation — Amount of a payment applied to one invoice (makes partial payments explicit).
  • RegisterPaymentRequest non-persistent — Wizard data: register a payment for one or more posted invoices.
  • CreditNoteRequest non-persistent — Wizard data: reverse a posted invoice into a credit note.
  • InvoiceAnalysis view entity — Posted invoice lines with untaxed/total amounts and residual per partner/product/period.

Associations inside the module (7): Invoice_ReversalOf, InvoiceLine_Invoice, Payment_Invoices, PaymentAllocation_Payment, PaymentAllocation_Invoice, RegisterPaymentRequest_Invoices, CreditNoteRequest_Invoice

Cross-module associations (16), all pointing down the layer stack: Invoice_Partner → Partners.Partner, Invoice_DeliveryAddress → Partners.Partner, Invoice_Salesperson → Administration.Account, Invoice_PaymentTerm → Core.PaymentTerm, Invoice_Currency → Core.Currency, Invoice_BankAccount → Partners.BankAccount, Invoice_SalesOrders → Sales.SalesOrder, Invoice_PurchaseOrder → Purchasing.PurchaseOrder, InvoiceLine_Product → Products.Product, InvoiceLine_UoM → Products.UoM, InvoiceLine_Taxes → Core.Tax, InvoiceLine_SalesOrderLines → Sales.SalesOrderLine, InvoiceLine_PurchaseOrderLine → Purchasing.PurchaseOrderLine, Payment_Partner → Partners.Partner, Payment_Currency → Core.Currency, Payment_BankAccount → Partners.BankAccount

  1. Team ownership — will different people/teams own sales, purchasing, inventory, invoicing? (POC default: one owner → boundaries by bounded context only)
  2. Reuse intent — any module you want to lift into another app later (e.g. Partners, Products)?
  3. Release cadence — must anything ship independently? (POC default: no)
  4. Security / regulatory segregation — any data that must be isolated beyond the 9 roles (e.g. invoicing only for finance)?
