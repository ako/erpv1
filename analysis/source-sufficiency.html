DimensionRatingEvidenceWhat the build must invent
nfrSecurity, performance, availability, retention, data volumesabsentNo performance, availability, retention or volume statements anywhere in the code or manifests; only implicit indexes (index=True) and record rules.POC: nothing to invent beyond Mendix defaults. Record as an explicit non-goal rather than a gap.
data_migrationExisting data, its volume and shape, and how cutover happensnamedOnly demo/seed data: demo/*.xml, data/template/*-generic_coa.csv (generic chart of accounts, taxes), uom/product categories in data/*.xml. No production data — Odoo here is a reference, not a system being retired.No data migration (POC, intake Q2/Q5). Seed data (UoM, currencies, chart of accounts, taxes) is reused as Mendix demo/startup data.
integrationsExternal systems, protocols, credentials, and behaviour when they failnamedControllers (portal, download docs), mail templates (data/mail_template_data.xml), payment providers (account_payment, not imported), EDI/e-invoicing hooks in account_move_send.py, barcode nomenclature (not imported).All out of POC scope; the build stubs or omits them. Email sending of quotes/invoices, online payment and portal are declared not implemented.
actorsWho uses the system, and where one role's authority stopsspecifiedres.groups records in each addon's security/*_security.xml: Sales User (own docs) / User (all docs) / Administrator; Purchase User/Administrator; Inventory User/Administrator; Invoicing/Billing/Accountant; base Internal/Portal/Public user. implied_ids give the hierarchy.Nothing to invent; Stage 3 maps groups to Mendix module/user roles. Portal and public users are out of scope (no portal addon).
processesFlows and sequences: what happens after what, and what state it leaves behindspecifiedState machines as fields.Selection 'state' + action_* methods: sale.order (draft→sent→sale, cancel), purchase.order (draft RFQ→sent→to approve→purchase→done/cancel), stock.picking (draft→waiting→confirmed→assigned→done), account.move (draft→posted→cancel). Bridges sale_stock/purchase_stock create pickings on confirm; stock_account values moves.Flow is in Python method bodies, not declared: the extractor can list states and action methods, but the sequence and side effects need reading per method (Stage 1/2). Approval steps (purchase 'to approve' double validation) are candidates for Mendix workflows.
rulesBusiness rules, validations, thresholds, calculations, eligibility criteriaspecified@api.depends computes (amounts, taxes, qty_delivered/invoiced), @api.constrains, _sql_constraints, onchanges, pricelist rules (product.pricelist.item), tax computation (account/models/account_tax.py), reservation strategy (stock FIFO/removal).Rules are complete but often generic/framework-heavy (account_tax._compute_taxes_for_single_line etc.). For a POC, Stage 2 must pick a simplified rule set (e.g. one tax per line, price-included flag) and record it as a deliberate simplification.
security_modelThe actor-to-permission mapping: who may do each thing, and who may notspecifiedsecurity/ir.model.access.csv per addon (model × group × CRUD) plus ir.rule record rules (e.g. sale 'Personal Orders' rule restricting salesman to own orders).Directly mappable to Mendix entity access + XPath constraints; the extractor should parse the CSVs and rules.
tenancyWhether one deployment serves many organisations, and how their data is isolatedspecifiedMulti-company is pervasive: company_id fields, _check_company_auto, ir.rule records with company_ids in */security/*.xml.Multi-company is out of scope (confirmed scope); the build uses a single company. Fields company_id are dropped or kept as a single Core.Company reference — Stage 3 decision.
uiScreens, navigation, layout, and which data appears on whichspecified176 views/*.xml + wizard/report xml: form/list/kanban/search/pivot/graph views per model, menuitem trees per app (Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Invoicing), actions with domains/contexts.Screens are specified as Odoo views, not wireframes; Stage 3 design must translate them into Mendix pages (Atlas) rather than replicate Odoo's web client. Kanban/pivot need Mendix-native equivalents or are dropped.
domainEntities, their attributes, and the relationships between themverifiedORM models are the executable definition: ~300 _name models across 13 addons (base/models/res_partner.py, product/models/product_template.py, sale/models/sale_order.py, stock/models/stock_move.py, account/models/account_move.py ...), fields with types, required, relations, _inherit/_inherits.Nothing missing, the opposite: too much. Stage 3 must simplify (Odoo-inspired, Mendix-native) — choose which of ~300 models become entities; ~95 transient wizards become non-persistent entities or pages.
